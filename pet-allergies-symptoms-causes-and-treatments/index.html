Pet allergies: symptoms, causes, and treatments

Originally published 2025-04-22 · Original hospital article

Understanding allergies

Not only may allergies affect people, but they can also affect our pets. Pet allergies may be both upsetting and frustrating for both pets and their owners, whether they manifest as itchy skin, watery eyes, or persistent ear infections. The good news? Allergies can be efficiently treated with the right diagnosis and treatment.

Common symptoms

Common Symptoms of Pet Allergies:

  • Persistent scratching, licking, or biting
  • Red, inflamed, or flaky skin
  • Hair loss or hot spots
  • Chronic ear infections
  • Watery eyes or sneezing
  • Digestive issues like vomiting or diarrhoea (in food allergies)

Causes of allergies

What Leads to Allergies in Pets?

Environmental allergies

Pet allergies typically fall into one of three categories:
Atopy, or environmental allergies, can be brought on by dust mites, mould, pollen, and even grass. Although they can happen all year round, these are frequently seasonal.

Food and flea allergies

1. Food Allergies: Dairy, wheat, beef, and chicken are common causes. Usually, food allergies manifest as gastrointestinal distress or skin problems.
2. Flea Allergy Dermatitis: A single flea bite can result in severe skin inflammation and itching in certain pets that are very sensitive to flea saliva.

Treatment options

Options for Treatment:
Finding the trigger is the first step in managing pet allergies. Your veterinarian can suggest flea management, an elimination diet, or allergy testing. Treatment options could consist of:

  • Antihistamines or corticosteroids
  • Prescription allergy medications or immunotherapy
  • Medicated shampoos and skin supplements
  • Specialized diets
