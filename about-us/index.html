DUNDAS EAST ANIMAL HOSPITAL

About Us

Serving Mississauga since 2010

Since opening its doors in 2010, Dundas East Animal Hospital has been dedicated to delivering high-quality veterinary care to pets in the Mississauga community. With a focus on compassionate service and personalized treatment, the hospital has earned a trusted reputation among pet owners seeking reliable and professional care.

Our services and technology

The experienced veterinary team at Dundas East Animal Hospital offers a full range of services, from preventive health check-ups and vaccinations to surgical procedures and diagnostics. Their approach emphasizes early detection and effective treatment, helping pets lead longer, healthier lives. Equipped with modern technology and a well-trained staff, the hospital is committed to maintaining the highest standards in veterinary medicine.

A personal approach

What sets Dundas East Animal Hospital apart is its client-centered philosophy. The staff understand the important role pets play in the lives of their owners, and they strive to create a welcoming, stress-free environment for both animals and their humans. They take time to communicate clearly, answer questions, and involve pet owners in every step of their pet’s care.

Our community relationships

Over the years, the hospital has built strong relationships with the community through its dedication to honest advice, expert care, and a genuine love for animals. Whether it’s a routine visit or a complex medical concern, the team ensures each pet is treated with respect, kindness, and professionalism.

A long-term partner in pet health

Dundas East Animal Hospital remains a reliable partner in pet health, proudly serving families and their furry companions with warmth and integrity for over a decade.
