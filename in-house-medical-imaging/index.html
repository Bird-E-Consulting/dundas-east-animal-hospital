In-House Medical Imaging

About medical imaging

Medical imaging offers a non-invasive way to gain valuable insights into your pet’s health. Our skilled team of imaging professionals is experienced in using advanced diagnostic equipment to support accurate and timely assessments.

X-rays, ultrasound and ECG

We proudly offer a range of imaging techniques on-site for your convenience. 

Our in-house imaging services include:

  • Radiography (X-rays): X-rays provide detailed images of areas with contrasting tissue densities, making them ideal for evaluating bones, joints, and certain soft tissues.
  • Ultrasound: This imaging method uses sound waves to create real-time, two-dimensional images of internal organs, helping to assess the heart, abdomen, and reproductive system.
  • Electrocardiogram (ECG): This test measures the electrical activity of the heart, aiding in the diagnosis of heart rhythm abnormalities and other cardiac conditions.

Our diagnostic approach

Our goal is to provide accurate diagnostics in a comfortable, stress-free environment, helping ensure the best possible care for your pet.
