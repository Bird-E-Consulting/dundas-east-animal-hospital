Understanding common skin conditions in pets

Originally published 2025-04-22 · Original hospital article

Pets frequently visit the veterinarian for skin problems, and with good cause. The largest organ in your pet and a major sign of general health is their skin. If left untreated, an abnormality can lead to discomfort, tension, and possibly major problems.
1. One of the main causes of inflamed, itchy skin is allergies. Certain foods, fleas, pollen, and even dust mites can cause allergies in pets. Excessive licking, scratching, or red, swollen skin are symptoms. Finding the allergen and controlling it with medication, dietary modifications, or environmental changes might provide much-needed relief.
2. Infestations of fleas are another major offender. Sensitive pets may experience significant reactions after just one flea bite. It’s crucial to regularly prevent fleas, particularly in the summer months.

3. Scratching or licking can quickly result in the development of hot spots, which are raw, moist patches of skin. In order to stop infection and encourage healing, these require immediate care.
4. Both humans and pets can contract infectious fungal illnesses like ringworm. Common symptoms include red circular sores, scaly areas, and hair loss. Stopping the spread requires accurate diagnosis and treatment.
5. A bad diet, harsh shampoos, or environmental factors like dry air can all contribute to dry skin and dandruff. To restore the health of your skin, your veterinarian may suggest adjustments or supplements.

Don’t wait if you observe your pet scratching more than normal or if their skin or coat changes. Our goal at [Dundas East Animal Hospital] is to identify, care for, and ensure that your pet is literally at ease in their own skin.
