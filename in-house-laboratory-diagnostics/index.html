About in-house testing

In-house laboratory services provide quick, reliable insights into your pet’s health—especially when a physical examination alone doesn’t reveal the full picture. These diagnostic tools help veterinarians make accurate assessments and start treatment without delay.

Results and timely care

Having diagnostics performed on-site means you don’t have to wait for results to return from outside labs. In most cases, results are available within an hour, allowing for immediate action and timely care.

Early detection

Laboratory testing is essential for the early detection of various health conditions such as liver and kidney disease, diabetes, urinary tract infections, parasitic infestations, and more. Early diagnosis often leads to better outcomes and faster recovery times.

Available tests and reference laboratories

A wide range of diagnostic tests are available in-house for your convenience. If a specific test isn’t available on-site, coordination with trusted reference laboratories ensures your pet receives the necessary care.
