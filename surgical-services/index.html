Surgical Services

Our skilled veterinarians offer a wide range of surgical services, from routine procedures to more advanced surgeries. To ensure the best possible outcomes for our patients, we occasionally refer cases to specialists—board-certified veterinary surgeons—when more advanced training or equipment is required. We prioritize your pet’s safety and comfort, taking all necessary precautions to provide the highest level of care. This includes performing a physical exam and pre-anesthetic testing before surgery, closely monitoring your pet during the procedure, and administering pain relief to ensure a smooth recovery.

Our team is pleased to offer care in the following areas. For a complete list of available services, please contact our office:

  • Spay Procedures
  • Neuter Procedures
  • Orthopedic Surgeries
  • Soft Tissue Surgeries
  • Cryosurgery
  • Cystotomy
