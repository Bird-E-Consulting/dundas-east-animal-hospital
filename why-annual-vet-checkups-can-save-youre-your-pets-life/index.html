Why annual checkups matter

You want your pet to have a long, happy, and healthy life because you are a pet parent. Making an appointment for yearly veterinarian examinations is one of the best ways to guarantee that. These visits have the potential to save lives, so they are more than just regular.
Because they mature far more quickly than people, pets may experience rapid changes in their health. Your veterinarian can identify early symptoms of illness that might not be apparent at home with an annual visit. Cancer, diabetes, kidney disease, and arthritis are among the conditions that frequently begin quietly. Many of these problems can be successfully treated with early discovery, enhancing your pet’s quality of life and possibly extending their lifespan.

What happens during a wellness exam

Your veterinarian will conduct a wellness exam, which includes a complete physical examination, vital sign and weight checks, immunisation updates, and sometimes blood or urine testing. These tests aid in determining any underlying problems and creating a baseline of health for later use.
Exams are also the ideal time to talk about your pet’s food, behaviour, dental health, and any changes you’ve noticed. Weight management, dental cleanings, and parasite control are examples of preventive care that can keep minor problems from developing into major health difficulties.

Planning the next visit

At [Dundas East Animal Hospital], we think that the best way to maintain your pet’s health is to provide preventative care. Even when your pet appears to be doing well, yearly visits guarantee they get the care they need.
Schedule your pet’s yearly wellness examination now to give them the best chance at living a long, active life. It’s worth it for your furry family member!
