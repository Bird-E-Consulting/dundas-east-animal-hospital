Why preventive care matters

Taking a proactive approach to your pet’s health is one of the most loving things you can do. Preventive care lays the foundation for a long, happy life. With routine wellness exams, we can spot potential health concerns early and treat them before they become serious or life-threatening.

Wellness visits and available services

During a wellness visit, your veterinarian will conduct a thorough physical exam and discuss your pet’s diet, activity level, and overall well-being. Based on the exam and your pet’s health history, we’ll recommend tailored preventive care — which may include vaccinations, parasite prevention (against both external threats like fleas, ticks, and ear mites, and internal ones like worms and heartworm), dietary guidance, coat and skin maintenance, joint support, weight management, or dental care.

We proudly offer a full range of wellness and preventive services, including:

  • Comprehensive wellness exams
  • Vaccinations
  • Puppy and kitten wellness programs
  • Diabetic management
  • Flea and tick prevention
  • Nutritional counselling
  • Travel certificates and documentation
  • Medical grooming
  • Spay and neuter surgeries
  • Professional dental cleaning and extractions
